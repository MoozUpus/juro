# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

JURO serves people in Uzbekistan who need help with personal, family, employment, housing, debt, contract, fine, and consumer matters. It also serves entrepreneurs, startups, small and medium businesses, companies without a permanent in-house lawyer, and legal or administrative teams.

## Product Purpose

JURO is a digital LegalTech platform built around one continuous legal workflow. A user can describe a situation, receive a structured explanation, create or review a document, understand risks and next steps, and involve a human specialist when needed.

## Positioning

JURO is not a general-purpose AI chat. It connects legal conversations, documents, risks, action plans, history, and human support inside one personal legal context.

## Operating Context

The public website explains the product and routes users into the application at `app.juro.uz`. The product supports Russian and Uzbek. Users may arrive under stress, on a mobile device, and without legal training, so language and next actions must remain direct.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed public product routes include registration, sign-in, AI chat, document creation, document review, action plans, and consultations.
- The document creation production route is `/document-builder`; the public site must not link to an obsolete `-test` route.
- Some human-service, voice, Jurobek, and collaboration capabilities remain in development and must be labelled honestly.
- AI output can contain errors and does not replace mandatory professional, notarial, judicial, or government procedures.
- The production home page must remain unchanged while `/landing-test` is being evaluated.
- The test route must not be indexed.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: JURO.
- Tagline: «Юрист в кармане».
- Core idea: «Юридическая помощь, документы и понятные действия — в одном месте».
- Existing logo and Jurobek assets must be preserved.
- Jurobek is a young Central Asian digital assistant in a traditional Uzbek tubeteika, used as a trustworthy guide rather than a toy mascot.
- Core colors: deep navy around `#062844`, gold around `#BE974F`, and a warm light ground around `#F8F6F2`.
- Tone: clear, calm, legally careful, warm, and confident.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing production landing page and application routes.
- RU and UZ legal-information pages.
- Existing JURO logos, favicons, Open Graph image, and transparent Jurobek asset.
- No confirmed customer testimonials, partner logos, security certificates, licenses, awards, or performance claims may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Show a useful legal outcome, not a list of AI technologies.
- Keep the user in control of facts, documents, access, and final decisions.
- Make uncertainty and product status visible.
- Preserve one continuous context instead of forcing users to start over.
- Connect AI assistance to human review when the situation requires it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public experience targets WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, touch targets around 44px, readable RU and UZ copy, and layouts that remain usable from 320px upward.
