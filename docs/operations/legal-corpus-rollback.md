# Legal corpus rollback

Rollback the Indexed Official Corpus by atomically selecting the immediately prior capability-compatible Activation Set. Verify its immutable R2 BM25 manifest, dense index contract, exact membership reconciliation and body-free D1 release roots before resuming indexed traffic.

Never roll back by:

- editing release membership or legal text;
- overwriting a sparse segment or reusable embedding;
- pairing current/history or sparse/dense components from different releases;
- restoring retired D1 body/posting schemas;
- reattaching Qdrant or Cloudflare AI Search; or
- changing DNS or deleting migrations.

If no complete prior Candidate Packet is available, mark indexed retrieval unavailable and continue the strict Source Ladder through Live Official Search. Preserve the failed and prior releases, activation event and bounded verification evidence. The route-free legal-corpus Worker has no acquisition cron or legacy write path to restart.
