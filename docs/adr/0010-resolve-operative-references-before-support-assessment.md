# Resolve operative references before support assessment

Status: accepted

An explicit reference within an already identified official instrument should not require rediscovering that instrument through embedding and keyword searches. That extra round adds latency and can displace evidence for an independent requirement.

A Reference Lookup projects accepted citation identities into a mapping from Text Revision, language and article identifier to candidate keys. Its immutable root is bound to the pinned Search Release and original membership inventory. When membership uses generic citation labels, construction authenticates the accepted article metadata and joins it by the exact evidence rendition and language. Construction checks identity and ordinal uniqueness and reports non-article records separately. The lookup root records the metadata manifest hash; runtime independently checks the article identifier in the authenticated provision bytes before assessment. Source artifacts and applied migrations remain unchanged.

Before support assessment, retrieval may follow at most three unresolved explicit references within the same instrument. Lookup results are discovery candidates only. They must pass the existing independent membership, temporal, controlling-evidence and final claim checks. They do not establish Requirement Support or automatically enter the answer. Reference discovery preserves the initial selection pool; additional candidates have their own bounded allowance.

Missing or unavailable reference discovery leaves the existing bounded semantic repair available. Ambiguous references, references to other instruments and broad article ranges do not become inferred links. Acceptance requires full projection parity, corruption and revision-isolation checks, and repeated local browser inspection of meaning, citations and complete-answer latency.

Assessment groups explicitly linked provisions within its existing bounded batches. A generally worded provision can establish the grounds incorporated by a narrower referring rule; both texts must be visible to assess that relationship. References crossing a batch boundary provide bounded context in either direction. Context never creates a support mapping automatically, and every candidate still receives independent assessment. The planner's named personal statuses and forums remain part of their respective requirements and search formulations, rather than being discarded after query generation.
