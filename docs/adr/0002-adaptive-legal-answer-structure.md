# Present legal answers through an adaptive product-owned structure

Every substantive Legal Answer uses localized product-owned sections for the Main Point, What the Law Says, and What to Do Next. Important Considerations, Deadlines, What to Prepare, and Additional Materials appear only when relevant; this keeps answers predictable and readable without manufacturing empty content or allowing model-authored headings to drift between responses.

## Consequences

The structured response schema remains the source of presentation truth. Markdown may format content inside a section, but it does not choose the section hierarchy. Detailed answers show all substantive sections expanded, Official Citations identify validated authority inline through product-rendered Lex.uz links, and narrow follow-ups omit sections they do not need. Case Facts remain separate from legal propositions and source-freshness states; neither an article number nor an operational warning may become a proposed Case Fact. When validated evidence cannot support a conclusion, JURO presents an Insufficient-Evidence Result instead of rendering an empty or speculative Legal Answer.
